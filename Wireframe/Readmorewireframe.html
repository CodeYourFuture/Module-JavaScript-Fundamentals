Purpose of Wireframe

A purpose of a wireframe to align stakeholders on the project's vision, facilitate early feedback, and prevent costly, time-consuming revisions during the development phase.

Key Purposes

  • Structure & Layout: Defines where elements like headers, text, images, and buttons will go.
  • Functionality: Shows interactive elements (forms, links) and how users navigate through the product.
  • User Experience (UX): Maps out the user journey and content hierarchy to ensure intuitive use.
  • Collaboration: Provides a shared visual reference for designers, developers, and clients to discuss and approve the core design.
  • Early Feedback: Enables quick testing and iteration on core structure before investing in visual design, saving resources.
